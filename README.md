# Common Phrases — English & Uzbek Flashcards (A1–A2)
### Kundalik va Dars Iboralari (O'zbekcha tarjimasi va Audio bilan)

A clean, modern, touch-friendly web application featuring 200 essential everyday & classroom English phrases with Uzbek translations and realistic neural pronunciation audio.

---

## 🌟 Key Features

1. **3D Flip Flashcards with Mobile Swipe**:
   - Tap anywhere on the card or hit <kbd>Space</kbd> to flip between English and Uzbek.
   - **Touch Gestures on Mobile**: Swipe left for Next card, swipe right for Previous card!
   - **Front**: Clean English phrase, category badge, card number, and prominent audio player.
   - **Back**: Clear Uzbek meaning, repeat audio button, and Mastered/Review buttons.

2. **Realistic Neural Audio for All 200 Phrases**:
   - 200 pre-rendered crystal-clear MP3 files in [`audio/`](file:///Users/ahmetyadgarov/.gemini/antigravity/scratch/curator-flashcards/audio).
   - Adjustable playback speed (`0.8x`, `1.0x`, `1.2x`).
   - Auto-speak toggle and continuous slideshow autoplay.

3. **Side Drawer for Categories & Search**:
   - Tap **`Categories`** in the header or the category chip on the card to open the slide-out drawer.
   - Built-in instant live search across both English and Uzbek.
   - Filters: All Phrases (200), Classroom Phrases (140), Attendance & Late (60), Mastered ⭐, Review ⚠️.

4. **100% Responsive & Mobile-Optimized**:
   - Dynamic typography (`clamp`) that fits phone screens without overflow.
   - Ergonomic 48px+ touch targets designed for comfortable thumb use.
   - Works seamlessly on iPhone, Android, tablets, and desktops.

5. **3 Study Modes**:
   - 🎴 **Cards**: 1-by-1 card flip study mode.
   - 📋 **All Phrases**: Complete grid list with 1-click audio and translation reveal.
   - 🎯 **Quiz**: Interactive multiple choice quiz with instant feedback and score tracker.
